# ADR 0027: Inter is bundled, and icons are SVG files that an engine colors by token

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The icons of the editor had three faults (see the [UI and UX plan](../../planning/ui-ux-plan.md), V2, V3, V5). The 38 icons of actions were one blue `QIcon` each, made by painting one pixmap at the default size of the SVG, so a switch of theme could not recolor them and a screen with a device pixel ratio of 2 showed them soft. Their color failed the contrast rule on the light theme (1.66 to 1). Their origin and license were not recorded: several file names match the Material Design Icons set. The text used the font of the platform, which gives three looks and different metrics on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The owner decided (2026-11-06) to follow the recommendations of the plan: Tabler for the icons of actions, Inter as the font, and no dependency on a library of widgets. The plan marked three things as not done: a frozen build, the license text of the exact files, and the stroke at 125 and 150 percent.

## Decision

- **Inter, one variable file.** `src/aas_workingbench/fonts/Inter-Variable.ttf` is Inter 4.1 (name table: "Version 4.001;git-66647c0bb"), copied unmodified from the Google Fonts repository (folder `ofl/inter`, commit `e1d6480102fed30739fead0faee463101f892c8f`, file `Inter[opsz,wght].ttf`, 876,576 bytes, SHA-256 `29160a80ff49ddcab2c97711247e08b1fab27a484a329ce8b813d820dc559031`). It was renamed only to keep brackets out of a file name. It has the axes `wght` 100 to 900 and `opsz` 14 to 32, and covers Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, and Vietnamese. `OFL.txt` next to it is `LICENSE.txt` of release 4.1 of the font, SIL Open Font License 1.1, with "Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors". The license has no Reserved Font Name, so the file may be bundled under its own name. The Google Fonts copy has "2020" in its first line and the same text otherwise.
- **The application font is Inter at the body size of the type ramp.** `ui/theme/fonts.py` registers the file and sets `QApplication.setFont` to Inter, 13 pixels, Regular. This also replaces the fonts that the platform gives to menus and tooltips. `__main__.py` does it right after the `QApplication` is made, before the first module of the editor is imported, because `DEFAULT_FONT` reads a `QFont` at import. `DEFAULT_FONT` also names the family itself, so the order cannot change what the trees get. A file that fails to load is logged, and the system font stands in for it.
- **Zoom is untouched.** The size of the rows is a point size that the models keep on their font (`fontSizeFilesView`, `fontSizeDetailedView`, default 12 pt, and the zoom actions that add or remove 2). Only the family and the weight of that font changed. The type ramp is read by the code that sets a font, and the style sheet still sets no font, so the zoom still wins (ADR 0025). The migration of the default size is phase F.
- **Tabler for icons of actions.** `src/aas_workingbench/icons/tabler/` holds 53 outline icons of release 3.50.0 of Tabler Icons, unmodified, with the `LICENSE` of that release (MIT, "Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna"). 38 are the icons of actions, and 15 are the icons that the plan lists as new (`chevron-right`, `chevron-down`, `search`, `circle-x`, `alert-triangle`, `circle-check`, `link`, `command`, `sun`, `moon`, `lock`, `eye`, `layout-bottombar`, `key`, `dots`), for the phases that need them. The 38 old files, of unrecorded origin, were removed. Only the logos remain in `icons/`.
- **An engine colors an icon by token when it draws.** `ui/theme/icons.py` has `ThemedIconEngine`, a `QIconEngine` that holds the path of an SVG file that draws in `currentColor` and a role. It replaces `currentColor` with the color of the role in the theme in use, in the state that Qt asks for: `fg_disabled` when disabled, `fg_primary` under the pointer and in a selected row for the default role, and the color of the role otherwise. The roles (`IconRole`) are `DEFAULT` (`fg_secondary`), `MUTED`, `ACCENT`, `ERROR`, `WARNING`, and `SUCCESS`. A small test measures every role on every surface at 3 to 1 in both themes. The theme is read when the icon draws, so one `QIcon` made at import follows a switch of theme, and the constants of `settings/icons.py` keep their names and stay global objects.
- **High resolution.** The engine draws the SVG at the size that Qt asks for times the device pixel ratio, for `pixmap`, `scaledPixmap`, and `paint`, and sets the ratio on the pixmap. Pixmaps are kept in `QPixmapCache`, which Qt owns and bounds, so rows draw an icon once. They are not kept in a Python cache, because a pixmap that outlives the `QApplication` is unsafe. No `QT_` variable is set.
- **The way to add a kind.** The icons of the AAS kinds are files of one color that draw in `currentColor`, made with `svg_icon(path, role)`. Phase G made them: see the note at the end.
- **The build.** `aas_workingbench.spec` adds `src/aas_workingbench/fonts`, and `NOTICE`, so that the license of the font and the attributions travel with the build. `src/aas_workingbench/icons`, already bundled, brings `tabler/` and its `LICENSE`. The old theme lines stay until the phase that removes the themes.
- **A defect found on the way.** `DEFAULT_FONT` had `setWeight(40)`. 40 was Normal in Qt 5, and in Qt 6 it is below Thin. With a system font this picked its lightest style, and with Inter, which has a Thin style, every row would draw as a hairline (measured: 28 percent of the ink of Regular, on the `windows` and `offscreen` platforms). It is `QFont.Weight.Normal` now.

## Consequences

- Action icons are 2 pixel strokes on a 24 grid, drawn at 16, so 1.33 pixels wide at scale 1 and not aligned to the pixel grid. They look softer than a hand tuned set at 100 percent, and sharp at 200 percent. 125 and 150 percent were not looked at.
- The text of the interface is Inter, which is wider than Segoe UI at the same size. A window or a dialog that was sized to Segoe may need room. The existing tests that measure with the real font still pass.
- Two icons of the old set that the plan said to look at were looked at. `SAVE_ALL_ICON` is `stack-2`, which reads as layers and not as a floppy disk. The two autoscroll icons, `focus-2` and `current-location`, are nearly the same at 16 pixels. Neither autoscroll icon is shown today: the code that would use them is commented out. `MIME_IMAGE_ICON` is `photo`, because the plan's candidate `file-type-jpg` writes `JPG`.
- The package grows by about 0.84 MiB for the font and 53 small icons, and shrinks by the 38 old icons.
- Text in other scripts (CJK, Hangul, Arabic, Thai) falls back to a font of the system, as the plan found on Windows. On Linux and macOS this was not tried.
- The I and l of Inter look alike, which matters in ids. A font feature that changes this was not tried.

## Note, phase G: the 24 kind icons

`src/aas_workingbench/icons/kinds/` holds 24 SVG files, one for each kind of the plan (section 4), which replace `getCharsIcon` and its letters in `Monospace`. They are this project's own drawings, under its license, and `NOTICE` says so. No set of icons was traced or copied, and no outline of a font is used.

- **Grid.** 16 by 16, a margin of 1 pixel, a stroke of 1.33, `currentColor`, no text, no style, no script. A test reads every file for this.
- **Shape is the family, letters are the kind.** A filled square is an identifiable (shell `A`, submodel `SM`, concept description `CD`). A filled slab is a part of one (asset information `AS`, submodel reference `SR`). A square with a band on top is a container (`SC`, `SL`, `E`). A pill is behavior (`OP`, `CA`, `EV`). A plain square is data (`P`, `ML`, `R`, `B`, `F`, `RF`). A square with a pointed side is a relation (`RE`, `RA`). A circle is an annotation (`Q`, `X`, `ID`, `K`, `DS`). Letters of an identifiable are knocked out of the fill, and the others are lines.
- **The letters are made here.** Each is a skeleton of lines and arcs, stroked at 1.33 (1.25 for the pairs inside an outline, to keep their counters open). They are written as paths. For the filled shapes Qt's `QPainterPathStroker` turned the skeleton into an outline, which is cut out with the even-odd rule, because Qt's SVG renderer has no masks.
- **Role.** `IconRole.ACCENT`, the blue of the application, in one constant (`KIND_ROLE`) of `ui/theme/kind_icons.py`.
- **A mapping by class.** `KIND_FILES` maps each class of the SDK to a file, and a subclass takes the file of its nearest base. A test walks the SDK for every concrete submodel element, identifiable and reference, so a new class without an icon fails it. `TYPE_SHORTS_DICT` is gone, and with it the generic `DT` fallback.
- **Differs from the plan.** The letters are capitals only: `AS`, `OP`, `CA`, `EV`, `RF`, `RE`, `ID`, because a small second letter does not read at 16 pixels. A container is a band on top and not a "double edge", because a second edge leaves no room for two letters in 16 pixels. The "error here" badge of the plan is not built.
- **Checked** on Windows, offscreen, with a contact sheet at 16 pixels at scale 1 and at 4 times, in the light and the dark theme (`docs/planning/ui-ux/kind-icons-sheet.png`, and the pixels of the 16 pixel icons enlarged 8 times with no smoothing in `kind-icons-real-size.png`). At the real size `SC`/`SL`, `RE`/`RA`, `ID` and `DS` are the least clear, and the shape and the Type column carry them. The plan's review by a modeler was **not** done, and nobody has judged the letters against the habits of people who model.

## Alternatives considered

- **Fluent System Icons** (MIT). Allowed, and more coherent with Fluent 2, but the plan chose Tabler to avoid the look of a Microsoft product. Only the names in `settings/icons.py` would change.
- **Lucide, Phosphor, Material Symbols.** Lucide and Phosphor were possible. Three names of Lucide were not found where expected. Material Symbols are filled, like the old set.
- **Segoe UI, Segoe UI Variable, Segoe Fluent Icons.** Not redistributable (plan, section 4).
- **A native font.** Free, but three looks and metrics to test, and nothing to carry the SemiBold of the type ramp on all systems.
- **One `QIcon` with a pixmap for each theme and state, made at a theme switch.** It would have to be remade for every icon, and every widget that kept a copy would show the old one. The engine reads the theme when it draws.
- **`QIcon.addPixmap` at several sizes.** Gives the right sizes only for the ratios that were listed. The engine draws the SVG at whatever Qt asks for.
- **Subsetting Inter.** It would save about 0.6 MiB and would be a Modified Version under the OFL, with its own checks, for little gain.
- **Registering the font in a style sheet or with `QFontDatabase` at import.** Both need the application, and the font has to be there before the modules that read `QFont()`. `__main__.py` is the place.
